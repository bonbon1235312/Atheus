№ 04 — The room

A converted fabric warehouse
on the corner of Tib Street.

Open kitchen along one wall, twenty-eight covers, four seats at the counter. Old brick on the back, a Welsh dresser holding the crockery, and a wine list that fits on one page.

№ 05 — The list

Twelve farms, sixty miles.

The people who actually grow, raise, catch or churn what arrives at the back door each morning. We don't buy from a wholesaler.

Pextenement Cheese · Littlewoods of Holmfirth · Mossbrook Farm · Out of the Blue · Saddleworth Mushrooms · Mr Kirkham's · Goosnargh Geese · Forager Yorkshire · Cinderhill Gardens · Pennine Game · Linthwaite Trout · Carr Farm Dairy
