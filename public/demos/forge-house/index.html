§ 01 — The room

A converted unit on Foundry Yard. Built for one thing.

Forge House sits in a converted Foundry Yard unit in Holbeck — open seven days a week and coached six. We are not a chain, not a circuit-class studio, and not a CrossFit box. We're a strength gym, and that's the whole pitch.

The 240-member ceiling is the point. Eight platforms, three lifters per platform at the busiest hour. You will not wait twenty minutes for a squat rack. You'll learn everyone's name inside a month.

§ 02 — Classes

Coached lifting. No random workouts.

Members mix and match these across the week. Everything is capped for coaching quality. Programming progresses weekly off your actual numbers — not a one-size template.

  1. 01

    Lift Club

    Four-person small-group strength sessions focused on squat, bench, deadlift and smart accessory work. Technique feedback every set, programme built around your numbers.

    8 / week

  2. 02

    Engine Room

    Intervals, sled pushes, carries, low-skill conditioning that makes you fitter without trashing recovery. 45 minutes, moves fast, scaled by coach.

    6 / week

  3. 03

    Open Gym

    Train on your own schedule with coaching staff nearby and a room that takes training seriously. Programme delivered through the member portal.

    7 days

  4. 04

    Mobility & Movement

    Soft-tissue work, breathwork and joint-by-joint mobility. Sunday mornings, included with every membership above Starter.

    1 / week

Classes are capped at 8 (Lift Club at 4). Bookings open six days in advance through the member portal.

§ 05 — Your first week

Seven days. No mystery.

A trial isn't a single class. It's a coached week so you actually know what membership feels like before you commit.

Day 1

Intro & screen

Sit down with Asha. Movement screen, training history, what you're trying to actually do. Forty minutes, no upsell.

Days 2 – 3

Two coached classes

A Lift Club and an Engine Room. You meet two of the three coaches and the kind of members who train here.

Days 4 – 6

Open-gym access

A short programme written specifically for your trial week. Train solo, coach on the floor if you need a check.

Day 7

Decide

Sit down with Rory. He maps your first twelve-week block, or you walk. Either is fine. No contract until day eight.
