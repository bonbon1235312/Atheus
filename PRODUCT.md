# atheus — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Multi-tenant Pro Clubs league platform. Each league owner runs their season from an admin workspace; their members and fans see a public league website at `<slug>.atheus.dev`. The public site is the product the end-user cares about — it is the league's home on the internet.

## Register
brand — the public league website IS the experience. Design is the product.

## Users
- **League owners / admins**: Run the league. Approve results. Configure the season. Use the workspace (admin).
- **Players / fans (primary public audience)**: Check fixtures, results, standings, and their own stats. Usually on a phone, post-match, at night. Expect a real sports product — not a tool, not a dashboard.

## Tone
Confident. Direct. No fluff. Sports media energy — like a good match programme or the Athletic. Never corporate, never startup-generic.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (Notion, Linear aesthetic)
- Esports cyberpunk (neon on black, diagonal slash grids)
- Pitch/trophy/green football clichés
- Fantasy sports pastels
- Glassmorphism decoration

## Strategic principles
- Every league has custom branding via CSS vars (`--league-primary`, `--league-secondary`, `--league-accent`, `--league-bg`, `--league-surface`, `--league-text`, `--league-muted`). The design must work across any colour scheme a league owner chooses.
- Data is the hero. Typography and spacing should make numbers feel authoritative, not decorative.
- The site must feel like a real sports product that a professional organisation would be proud to share.
