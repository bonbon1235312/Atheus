# Atheus — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Atheus is a technology company that builds premium SaaS products, automation platforms, and developer tools. The company site presents Atheus as a parent brand. Individual products (League, Club, BlackWall) solve focused operational problems.

The live platform product remains the multi-tenant League system: league owners run seasons from an admin workspace; members and fans use public league sites at `<slug>.atheus.dev`.

## Register
brand — the company marketing site communicates engineering quality. Design is the first impression of the company.

## Users
- **Primary:** Businesses, organisations, communities, developers, technical founders, startup teams
- **Secondary:** Prospective customers, collaborators, employers, investors, technical recruiters
- **Product operators (League):** League owners/admins and players/fans on public league sites

## Tone
Engineering-first. Professional. Calm. Confident. Minimal. Direct. Never loud, childish, gamer-themed, crypto, or "AI bro."

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS templates and purple gradient marketing
- EAFC / esports cyberpunk identity on the company site
- Oversized buzzwords ("revolutionising", "next generation", "powered by AI")
- Stock illustrations, clipart, floating blobs, decorative noise

## Strategic principles
- Atheus is the parent company. Products are the centrepiece (League, Sites, Club, BlackWall).
- Sites covers fully custom website builds with scoped price ranges.
- Communicate tangible value: automation, speed, reliability, scalability, UX.
- Marketing redesign must not break `/admin`, tenant subdomains, or public league sites.
- League public sites keep per-league CSS variable branding.
- Adding a future product should mean adding data + a product page, not redesigning the system.
